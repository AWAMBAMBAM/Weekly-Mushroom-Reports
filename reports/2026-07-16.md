## 4. Global developments

- **U.S.–Canada mushroom trade dispute:** On 2026-07-14, the U.S. Department of Commerce issued a preliminary affirmative anti-dumping determination on fresh mushrooms from Canada, proposing an 8.26% duty on most fresh mushrooms, with company-specific rates for Champ's Fresh Farms (8.71%), Highline Produce (11.80%), and Farmers' Fresh Mushrooms (2%). Mushrooms Canada publicly disputed the finding as far lower than the originally alleged 32–44% dumping margins. Final determination is expected around 2026-12-03. Sources: [Medicine Hat News, 2026-07-14](https://medicinehatnews.com/news/national-news/2026/07/14/u-s-moves-to-slap-more-tariffs-on-fresh-canadian-mushrooms/), [BNN Bloomberg, 2026-07-14](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/tariffs/2026/07/14/us-moves-to-slap-more-tariffs-on-fresh-canadian-mushrooms/), [RealAgriculture](https://www.realagriculture.com/2026/07/canadian-mushroom-sector-slams-new-u-s-anti-dumping-duties/).
  *Relevance caveat:* this dispute concerns fresh/table (Agaricus) mushroom trade rather than adaptogenic species directly, but it affects cost structure and cross-border logistics for North American growers (including Highline, which also produces specialty/organic mushrooms) — relevant as broader sector context rather than an adaptogenic-specific development.
