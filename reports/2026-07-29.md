## 3. Market Data / Sales Channels

- **"Stress-relief drinks drive next wave of functional beverage growth"** (BeverageDaily, 2026-07-29). Stress-relief/relaxation/mood-support beverages — many built on adaptogen blends (reishi, ashwagandha, rhodiola) — are flagged as the fastest-growing functional-beverage segment. Cited data: 23% of adults bought functional food/drink in Q1 2026, another 36% want to (59% combined interest); top desired outcomes are sleep support (24%) and stress reduction (22%). Overall functional beverage market: ~USD 151.8bn (2025) → ~USD 163.8bn (2026), 7.93% CAGR to 2031.
  Source: [beveragedaily.com](https://www.beveragedaily.com/Article/2026/07/29/stress-relief-drinks-drive-next-wave-of-functional-beverage-growth/)
