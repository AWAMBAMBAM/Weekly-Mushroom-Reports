## 3. Market Data / Sales Channels

**USDA finalizes first update to U.S. mushroom grade standards in ~60 years**
The USDA Agricultural Marketing Service published a final rule in the Federal Register on **2026-08-25** revising the U.S. Standards for Grades of Mushrooms — the first substantive revision since the standards were established on July 15, 1966. The new standards take effect **2026-09-24**. Changes include: a new grade for portabella mushrooms, size moved out of grade criteria into its own section, revised defect tolerances (including treatment of open veils), and updated terminology/defect-scoring guides. The revision follows a six-year effort led by the American Mushroom Institute (AMI). Grower Highline Mushrooms has publicly responded, reaffirming its existing quality commitments to retail/foodservice customers ahead of the change.
This is directly relevant to sales channels: grading/defect-tolerance changes affect what growers can market as "No. 1" grade, sizing disclosures, and buyer specifications industry-wide.
